Dave McClure grew up on a small Central Illinois dairy farm and left it to live in nearby Bloomington-Normal, where he earned an English degree from ISU.  Though trained as a secondary school teacher, he traded in that occupation for travel and later found a career helping youth and families succeed.  Before he retired, as Director of Youth Service Bureau of Illinois Valley, he built a writing shack in the backyard of his home in Ottawa, Illinois.  He lives there now with his wife Colleen.  She, along with his daughter Maureen, his son Dean, and his granddaughter June, are the loves of his life.  He’s in that shack now, tapping out this blurb about himself on a keyboard.
